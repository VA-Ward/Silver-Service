Why Silver Service

Built around the hour you don’t have

  • The VIP bag: no queue, no counter

    Join the VIP program, pick up a $10 weatherproof express bag and after that it’s drop and go. Hand the bag over, tell a staff member when you need it, and we process the order, apply any current discount, charge the card on file and text you when it’s ready.

  • 24-hour collection at Zetland

    Hydrocarbon cleaning is water-free, so nothing shrinks and colours hold. Wet cleaning uses biodegradable detergent with computer-controlled drying and finishing, far gentler than a domestic wash. Questions we get asked

Straight answers

Where are your Sydney stores and when are you open?

We have four stores: Barangaroo, Drummoyne, West Ryde and Zetland. Barangaroo, Drummoyne and West Ryde open at 7:30am on weekdays; Drummoyne and West Ryde also trade Saturday. Zetland is open seven days, with late trading on Thursday, and has a 24-hour garment ATM for after-hours drop-off and collection. Full addresses, phone numbers and hours are listed above.

How long does dry cleaning take?

It depends on the garment and how busy the store is, so tell us when you need it and we’ll confirm at drop-off. Curtains and window furnishings come back in 48 hours. If something is urgent, ask about our VIP Express service.

Can I drop off or collect outside store hours?

Yes, at Zetland. The East Village store has a 24-hour garment ATM, so you can drop off and collect whenever it suits you rather than working around trading hours.

Do you pick up and deliver?

Yes. Curtains and window furnishings are collected and returned across most Sydney suburbs, and bridal work has complimentary pick-up and drop-off across most of Sydney. Businesses can set up a scheduled valet run for uniforms and workwear. Send the form and we’ll confirm whether your address is on a run.

Can you clean and preserve a wedding dress after the day?

That’s a specialty. Our bridal arm, Bridal Silver Service, has spent more than twenty years cleaning, restoring and preserving bridal and eveningwear, and Sydney and Melbourne designers refer their clients to us. We can collect the gown from your honeymoon hotel so it’s handled before you fly out, and we guarantee our workmanship on the gown and its ornamentation.

How often should curtains be cleaned, and how long do they take?

About every two years. Left longer, the dirt sets and becomes harder to remove, and over time it damages the fabric. Cleaning takes 48 hours, and we can replace hooks and handle basic repairs while they’re with us. Note that we can’t guarantee rubber backing — sun exposure and age break it down regardless of how gentle the method is.

Is your cleaning safe for beaded, sequinned or delicate garments?

Yes. Hydrocarbon cleaning is water-free and gentler than traditional solvents, so there’s no shrinkage and no colour fade, and it leaves no odour. Wet cleaning uses biodegradable detergent with computer-controlled finishing. The most delicate pieces are cleaned by hand. We also repair beading and crystals in-house.

What happens if a stain won’t come out?

We’ll keep working on it for as long as there’s a safe way to improve the item, and we’ll tell you honestly when there isn’t. Our guarantee is that your garments and furnishings won’t be damaged by our process — we won’t promise every stain lifts, because no cleaner can.

Do you do alterations as well as cleaning?

We do — we’re tailors as well as dry cleaners. Hems, zips, buttons, taking in and letting out, hem tape, hand stitching, knitwear repairs, beading and crystal repair, leather and suede repairs, and bridal alterations. Bring the garment in and we’ll fit it properly rather than guessing.
